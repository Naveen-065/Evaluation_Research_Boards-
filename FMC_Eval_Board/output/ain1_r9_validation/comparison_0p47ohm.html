Calculation including U10

Using the previous measured source voltage of 0.539 V and the design value R31 = 1 kΩ: INA851 input gain = 1 + 6000/R31 = 7. The PCB leaves G02 pins open, so output-stage gain = 1. Overall differential gain = 7. R33, R34, R37 and R38 are series resistors and do not set this nominal DC gain.

I = 0.539 / (3300 + 0.470) = 163.3101 µA
Vshunt = I × 0.470 = 76.7557 µV
VADC,diff(signal) = 7 × Vshunt = 0.537290 mV
ADC LSB = 2 V / 4096 = 0.48828125 mV
Expected signal increment = 1.100370 counts

The ideal outputs at 1.5 V common mode would be about 1.500269 V and 1.499731 V. Actual offsets can move both these voltages and the ADC baseline.
